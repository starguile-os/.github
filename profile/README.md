# STARGUILE OS


We build sovereign presence software for the people who live in the house, not the platforms that watch it.

---

## What we make

**OS Lite** — a shared-sky app for households. Runs in the browser today, on your own hardware tomorrow. Free-forever tier. No ads. No behavioral profiling. No data brokers.

**SEER** — the weather station on the roof. Triages the outside world (mail, messages, maps) and decides what crosses your threshold.

**OS Core** — the sovereign edge node. Your climate. Your weather. Your culture. Your rules.

**Nimbus*** - Sovereign Home Metal.

## The Three Hard No's

Every line of code in this org obeys these:

1. **No pixel extraction.** Cameras stay on the device. Vectors only.
2. **No buffer storage.** Ambient audio is never stored, transmitted, or transcribed.
3. **No ambient listening.** The mic opens on consent and closes on completion.

If a feature can't be built inside these limits, we don't ship the feature.
